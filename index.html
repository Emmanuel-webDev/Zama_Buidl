How FHE Makes This Possible

Your vote is encrypted client-side using the Zama network's FHE public key before it leaves your browser. The contract accumulates encrypted votes using FHE.add(). After voting ends, Zama's threshold MPC decrypts only the aggregate tally. Individual votes are mathematically impossible to recover.
